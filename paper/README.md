# Bilingual manuscript drafts

The Chinese and English manuscripts are working drafts of the same method paper. They describe a carrier–marking-conditioned batch QR simulator, quantitative geometric damage coverage, sourced material imagery, and optional acquisition degradations. They do not report trained restoration-model results or validated physical-photo realism.

The public manuscript intentionally omits the four external-collection source images in Figure 2. Image-level republication rights are unresolved. Figures 1 and 3–6 are provided as PNGs, and self-contained Chinese and English HTML versions are included. The author's local working manuscript contains additional layouts and files that are outside this public release.
